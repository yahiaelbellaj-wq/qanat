Sleepless night 1 of 3 · Deadline crunch

01

Every deadline at once — and the bank statements arrive the night before.

Every deadline hits my team at once, and half my clients send their bank statements the night before.

— What we hear from managing partners

Why it hurts

  • VAT returns and payment are due 28 days after each period[1]; a late corporate-tax return costs AED 500 a month and unpaid tax accrues 14% a year[2]
  • Next wave: businesses under AED 50m must appoint an e-invoicing provider by 31 March 2027 and go live on 1 July 2027[3] — missing it costs AED 5,000 a month[4]
  • In KSA, ZATCA Wave 25 integration is due by 1 February 2027[5]
  • You can't hire your way out: “The UAE does not have accountants to spare” (ACCA)[6]

What we build

  • Compliance calendar for every client: VAT period + 28 days, CT year + 9 months, e-invoicing provider and go-live, licence and UBO updates
  • For law firms: hearing dates, POA renewals and client tasks
  • Automatic WhatsApp and email 21, 10 and 3 days before each date, listing exactly which documents are missing
  • Upload portal per client — documents never travel through WhatsApp
  • Status board: requested, received, in review, filed, paid

Your week after

Juniors stop chasing. Partners review instead of firefighting in the last 48 hours.

Sleepless night 2 of 3 · Collections

02

The work is done. The invoice is still open in July.

We do the work, then wait months to get paid — and chasing a client for fees feels like losing them.

— What we hear from managing partners

Why it hurts

  • In the UAE, 58% of B2B credit sales are paid late, on 47-day average terms, and bad debt is 8% of overdue invoices (all sectors)[7]
  • Firms end up in court for their own fees — including a roughly Dh1m judgment in Dubai[8]
  • Bookkeeping retainers creep in scope without being re-billed

What we build

  • Monthly retainers billed in advance, with a card or bank payment link
  • Invoices triggered at matter milestones
  • Polite reminders at day 3, 10 and 20 — from “accounts”, not from the partner
  • Partner alerted only at day 30
  • Aged-receivables dashboard

Your week after

Nobody has to remember to invoice, and partners only step in on the few accounts that need them.

Sleepless night 3 of 3 · Compliant growth

03

Referrals have plateaued. Online only brings people who want free advice.

Referrals got us here, but online I only get people who want free advice on WhatsApp.

— What we hear from managing partners

Why it hurts

  • UAE advocates may not promote themselves against the profession's ethics or through intermediaries (Decree-Law 34/2022, Art. 45)[9]
  • Dubai's Circular 1/2024 makes firms answerable for how staff and intermediaries solicit clients[10]
  • Accountants face price shopping: corporate-tax return fees run from Dh3,000 to Dh20,000[11]

What we build

  • Bilingual service pages built around triggers: “Missed the CT deadline?”, “E-invoicing by 31 March 2027”, VAT voluntary disclosure, debt recovery
  • Educational posts signed and approved by a partner — never self-praise
  • Search ads on high-intent queries only
  • Paid or deposit-based consultation booking that filters free-advice seekers
  • Intake form that captures conflict-check fields

Your week after

New enquiries arrive pre-qualified, with a paid first meeting — and nothing a regulator could object to.
