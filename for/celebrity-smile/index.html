01 · What a new patient sees today

Loved on Google.
Hard to reach online.

Your website, as it loaded on 27 October. 407 reviews at 4.9 say the care is excellent. Online, several of the ways a patient tries to reach you don’t work.

celebritysmileuae.com
  1. 01
    The email on your site does not receive mail.

    info@celebritysmileuae.com is shown on every page and on the appointment page. A message sent to it on 25 October came back: the mailbox does not exist. Anyone who writes in never reaches you.

  2. 02
    A phone button that does not call.

    The most visible button in the header shows your number with a phone icon, but it opens the appointment form. The “Call us today” number in the top bar is plain text, so on a computer there is no click-to-call.

  3. 03
    WhatsApp hidden in the footer.

    WhatsApp, the channel most of your patients use, is a small icon at the bottom of a long page, and on a computer it opens WhatsApp Web. No floating button, no prefilled message.

  4. 04
    Booking is a callback request.

    The form has no date or time and the phone number is optional, so your team chases each request. The site is in Arabic only, with no English version for expat and visiting patients.
