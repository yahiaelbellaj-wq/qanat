01 · What a new patient sees today

A Korean system.
Five phone numbers.

Your homepage, as it loaded on 27 October. What you offer is unique in Dubai: a doctor-led Korean rehabilitation team. The website hides it, and leaves a new patient guessing which number to call.

korehab.com
  1. 01
    Two WhatsApp numbers, five numbers in all.

    The floating WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp Web on +971 58 573 9651, while the footer icon goes to +971 52 952 8840. With two landlines and the Korean line, a new patient sees five numbers and no hint which one books.

  2. 02
    The booking form asks patients to type a date.

    The homepage form has a free-text “Appointment Date & Time” box, so every request needs a call back to find a real slot. The Contact page uses a second, different form, so requests arrive in two shapes.

  3. 03
    The Korean story sits below a generic hero.

    One PM&R consultant and 12 Korean-certified physiotherapists, with a Korean-language line: that is unique in Dubai, and it only appears on the About page. The homepage opens on “Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation Services”.

  4. 04
    Dated corners.

    The newest offer is from June 2022, the footer reads © 2025, and “Explore all our treatments here” opens a page that sends you back to the homepage in a new tab.
