01 · What a new parent sees today

A unique test.
A form with no slot.

Your homepage, as it loaded on 27 October. The BMI test is something no other centre in Dubai offers. The website makes a parent click twice, fill three screens, and still not get a time.

mathquotient.org
  1. 01
    The first click opens reviews, not the form.

    On the homepage today, the first click anywhere, including on “Book A FREE Session”, opens a Google-reviews pop-up. The booking form only appears on the second click.

  2. 02
    The free session has no time slot.

    The form asks three screens of questions (grade, how the child feels about maths, last score, personality, contact, country) but never offers a date or a time. Step one reads “BLEOW 70%”.

  3. 03
    Your tutors are invisible.

    The site holds a whole faculty, but the “Our Expert Faculties” and “Our Team” sections are switched off today. The only teacher a parent meets is you.

  4. 04
    Social proof frozen in 2024.

    The reviews widget says “based on 233 reviews” and its newest review is from October 2024. The footer reads ©2023, and the hero badge says 4.9 while Google shows 4.8 from 275 reviews.
