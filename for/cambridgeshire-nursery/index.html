01 · What a new parent sees today

Loved by parents.
Frozen in 2017.

Your homepage, as it loaded on 27 October. Parents give you 4.9 stars and your Facebook page is alive. The website hasn’t caught up, and a parent can’t book a tour without calling.

cambridgeshirenursery.com
  1. 01
    Tours by phone or walk-in only.

    The Admissions page invites parents to visit on weekdays, ideally 9 to 11 am, or to make a Saturday appointment, but there is no way to pick a slot online. The next step is a 1.6 MB PDF admission form from 2016, to print and fill.

  2. 02
    The news stopped in 2017.

    The newest post in News & Events is the Ripe Market of 26 October 2017. Meanwhile your Facebook page, 6,065 followers, posted last Friday: the life of the nursery is invisible on the site.

  3. 03
    The gallery has no pictures.

    The Gallery page shows one broken image and “Visit our Facebook Page for more activity pictures”. For a nursery with a garden, sand play and sensory rooms, it is the page parents use to picture their child’s day.

  4. 04
    WhatsApp opens WhatsApp Web.

    On a computer the green bubble opens web.whatsapp.com with a generic message, so your team still has to ask the child’s age and start date. Your 4.9 rating from 204 Google reviews appears nowhere; the testimonials are letters from 2017.
