01 · What a new patient sees today

French excellence.
Generic front door.

Your homepage, as it loaded on 27 October. The clinic is clearly exceptional. The website makes a new patient work harder than they need to, and hides what makes Versailles unique.

versaillesdentalclinic.com
  1. 01
    Booking is a request, not a slot.

    The appointment page asks for a “preferred date” and sends a form: the patient sees no availability and gets no instant confirmation. The “Book now” menu item leads nowhere, and the contact page opens a second, separate form.

  2. 02
    Your story lives on a second website.

    About us, the clinic, the team and Dr Caron’s profile sit on welcome.versaillesdentalclinic.com, a site with a different look. The “Know more” button on the homepage goes through a raw site123.me address to get there.

  3. 03
    Two ages on one homepage.

    “Founded in 2007 in Dubai” and, further down, “for over 35 years”. Both are true, Dr Caron’s career started in France, but side by side they read as a contradiction.

  4. 04
    A stock smile instead of Versailles.

    The first screen is a close-up stock smile and “Your dental clinic in Dubai”. The fleur-de-lis reception, the tapestries and Dr Caron only appear on the other site. And there is no French version, although you greet patients with “Bonjour”.
