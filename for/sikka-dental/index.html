01 · What a new patient sees today

Five stars.
One phone number.

Your homepage, as it loaded on 27 October. Patients clearly love the clinic. The website sends every one of them to the phone, and hides the team they come back for.

sikkadentalclinic.com
  1. 01
    No way to book online.

    Every route ends in a phone call: the hero button is “Call us now” and the number is repeated in a full-width band. The contact form asks only for a name, an email and a comment, so you cannot even call a form sender back.

  2. 02
    Sunday slots you can only ask for by phone.

    The site says Sunday is “open on an appointment basis”, a real advantage for working families. Nothing lets someone request a Sunday visit without calling during the week.

  3. 03
    A stock hero, the real team hidden inside.

    The homepage opens on a stock dentist and patient. Your team photo, the seven dentists and the “patients from 65+ countries” only appear on inner pages.

  4. 04
    Small signs the site hasn’t been touched.

    The footer still reads “© 2023”, the contact page says “Moday to Saturday”, and the three homepage testimonials don’t link to your 219 Google reviews.
