Sleepless night 1 of 3 · Insurance cash

01

Work done in June, paid in October — minus the rejections.

I did the work in June, I'll see the money in October, and a chunk gets rejected anyway.

— What we hear from clinic owners and medical directors

Why it hurts

  • Insurers have typically taken 90–120 days to pay providers[1]
  • Insurer payments can be 80–90% of a provider's cashflow[2]
  • In KSA, claim rejections are estimated at 15–25%, led by medical-necessity justification and coding (vendor estimate)[3]
  • Physio claims are often rejected when no progress report follows around the 5th session[4]

What we build

  • Pre-visit WhatsApp form: Emirates ID or Iqama, insurance card, plan — before the patient walks in
  • Pre-approval checklist per payer and per procedure
  • Physio session counter that alerts at session 4 to send the progress report
  • Rejection tracker by payer, reason code and doctor, with resubmission deadlines
  • Claims ageing by payer, in one dashboard

Your week after

Monday becomes a 15-minute rejection review by cause. We don't replace your RCM team — we give them a cockpit.

02

The 4 pm is empty again. The hygienist is paid anyway.

My 4 pm didn't show, and the implant plan I quoted last month never came back.

— What we hear from clinic owners and medical directors

Why it hurts

  • No-show rate around 21% in UAE primary care[5]
  • Mediclinic reports 20–30% depending on the season[6]
  • Reminders depend on reception having time; there is no waitlist, no recall and no follow-up on quotes

What we build

  • WhatsApp reminders D-1 and H-2 with one-tap confirm or reschedule
  • Unconfirmed slots released to a waitlist the same day
  • Recall flows: 6-month hygiene, vaccines, end of a physio plan
  • Treatment-plan follow-up at D+3, D+10 and D+30, with a Tabby, Tamara or payment link for self-pay

Your week after

Reception stops cold-calling. Gaps get refilled the same afternoon.

Sleepless night 3 of 3 · Marketing & DHA

03

Paying Google every month — and one wrong post away from the regulator.

I pay Google and Instagram every month and can't tell you which patients they brought.

— What we hear from clinic owners and medical directors

Why it hurts

  • Dubai's social-media standards (Sept 2026) require medical-director approval and an archive of every post that names the facility[7]
  • In KSA the MOH cites penalties up to SAR 10m and licence revocation[8]
  • Leads arrive by call, DM and WhatsApp with no attribution — agencies report clicks, not patients

What we build

  • Booking page per treatment, in Arabic and English
  • Call and WhatsApp click tracking
  • Cost-per-booked-patient dashboard
  • Library of compliant post and ad templates
  • Approval workflow: draft → medical director → archived

Your week after

You read one number every Monday: patients booked per dirham spent.
