  • Landing pages per intent — free zone, mainland, golden visa, MISA — with government fees shown apart from service fees
  • “Licence issued” sent back to Google Ads as an offline conversion

Sleepless night 2 of 3 · Renewals

02

Renewals are your margin — and you lose clients you didn't know were due.

Renewals are my margin, and every year I lose clients I didn't even know were due.

— What we hear from setup firm founders

Why it hurts

  • Clients pay the fines and blame you: late Emirates ID renewal is AED 20 a day (max. AED 1,000)[3], late work permits AED 200 a month (max. AED 2,000)[4]
  • Late trade-licence renewal is commonly cited at around AED 250 a month (vendor)[5]
  • Renewal is where price shoppers leave — reviewers complain renewal quotes came close to a new setup[6]

What we build

  • One register per client: trade licence, establishment card, visas, Emirates IDs, work permits, Ejari, insurance
  • Automatic WhatsApp and email at 60, 30 and 7 days before each expiry, with the quote and a payment link
  • Account manager alerted when a client doesn't reply
  • Dashboard of AED due in the next 90 days

Your week after

Renewals arrive as quotes already sent — not as angry calls about a fine.

Sleepless night 3 of 3 · Stuck files

03

Your team spends the day answering “any update?”

My team spends all day chasing passport copies and answering ‘any update?’ instead of closing files.

— What we hear from setup firm founders

Why it hurts

  • Bank accounts remain “the biggest hurdle” for new companies, with long waits[7]
  • In KSA, Qiwa, Muqeem and GOSI are intertwined — a block in one spills into the others[8]
  • Corporate service providers are AML-supervised: CSPs and auditors received 95 penalties worth over AED 4m in H1 2025[9]

What we build

  • Document checklist per client with an upload link — passport, photo, NOC, UBO, KYC
  • Status stages: name, initial approval, MOA, licence, establishment card, visas, bank
  • Automatic “next step” WhatsApp to the client at each stage
  • Internal board flagging files stuck for more than 7 days

Your week after

Clients stop chasing you because they always know the next step. KYC files are complete before an inspector asks.
