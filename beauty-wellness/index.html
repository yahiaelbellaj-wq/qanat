Sleepless night 1 of 3 · Empty slots

01

Thursday you turn people away. Tuesday your team scrolls.

Thursday I turn people away, Tuesday my girls sit on their phones, and the 3 pm keratin just didn't show up.

— What we hear from salon, spa and studio owners

Why it hurts

  • Salary, visa and rent are fixed — an unsold hour is lost for good
  • Filling gaps through a marketplace is expensive: in the UAE, Fresha takes a 50% one-time commission on each new marketplace client (min. AED 20), plus 4.90% + AED 0.75 per online payment[1]
  • Vendors estimate salon no-shows at 15–25% without automation (vendor)[2]
  • The calendar swings with Ramadan, Eid and a slow July–August[3]

What we build

  • Your own booking link — Instagram bio, Google profile, WhatsApp — so direct clients stop being marketplace clients
  • Deposit by payment link for first-timers and long services only
  • WhatsApp reminders D-1 and H-2 with confirm or reschedule
  • Waitlist that refills cancellations automatically
  • Off-peak offers to clients who are due for a visit, plus pre-Eid and back-to-school campaigns

Your week after

Quiet mornings get filled from your own client list — not from discounts to strangers.

Sleepless night 2 of 3 · Staff & clients

02

When she leaves, 200 clients follow her WhatsApp.

I pay the visa, the housing, the training — and when she leaves, her clients go with her.

— What we hear from salon, spa and studio owners

Why it hurts

  • Pay is a low base plus tiered commission (one Dubai listing: 10% rising to 17.5% by monthly sales) — monthly disputes and hours of Excel[4]
  • Occupational health cards are mandatory and missing cards feature in closures[5]
  • In KSA, women's beauty outlets are subject to 100% Saudization, including manager and reception roles[6]

What we build

  • One WhatsApp number owned by the business, with a shared inbox — no more personal phones
  • Client history owned by the salon, not the stylist
  • Commission and tips sheet calculated from your POS export
  • Per-staff dashboard: utilisation, rebooking rate, retail attach
  • “Meet your new stylist” message when someone leaves

Your week after

Payroll day becomes a check, not an argument — and clients stay when staff go.

Sleepless night 3 of 3 · Packages & retention

03

150 packages sold on White Friday. Nobody knows who has sessions left.

I sold 150 packages and I don't know who still has sessions, who expired, or who never came back.

— What we hear from salon, spa and studio owners

Why it hurts

  • Deal-site clients rarely come back for a second visit[7]
  • Prepaid packages are a liability: Abu Dhabi bans auto-renewal for gyms, requires a free 3-day trial and refunds if a gym closes for over a month[8]
  • Package balances live on paper or in the owner's head

What we build

  • Package and membership tracker with “3 sessions left” and expiry reminders
  • Renewal by WhatsApp payment link — no auto-renew needed
  • Win-back messages at 45, 60 and 90 days
  • Birthday messages and a review request after every visit
  • Tabby or Tamara for packages

Your week after

Every Monday you see outstanding sessions and the clients at risk of leaving.
