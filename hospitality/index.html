Sleepless night 1 of 3 · Platforms

01

The platforms keep your guests — and a third of the bill.

Talabat sends me orders but keeps the customer, and a third of the bill.

— What we hear from hotel and restaurant operators

Why it hurts

  • UAE operators cite delivery-app commissions of over 30%, plus packaging and discounts[1]
  • Booking.com starts around 15% and rises under Preferred Partner (guide)[2]; Airbnb moved to a 15.5% host-only fee[3]
  • With restaurant margins of 8–11%, a commission above the margin turns an order or a night into a loss[4]

What we build

  • Restaurants: commission-free ordering page (pickup and delivery, payment link) and a WhatsApp ordering flow
  • Loyalty offer for dine-in and website guests
  • Villas and hotels: direct-booking site with a booking engine and a “book direct” perk (late checkout, transfer)
  • Post-stay WhatsApp journey that brings opted-in repeat guests back direct

Your week after

Repeat guests stop paying the toll, and you build a list you keep.

Sleepless night 2 of 3 · Empty nights

02

Thursday was full on the book. Half didn't show.

Thursday was full on the book, half didn't show. And summer kills me.

— What we hear from hotel and restaurant operators

Why it hurts

  • Fine-dining venues in Dubai now charge Dh100–300 per guest for no-shows to protect seats[5]
  • Demand swings with summer, Ramadan and — in 2026 — the regional conflict: Dubai hotel occupancy fell to 56.4% in H1 2026[6]
  • Bookings aren't confirmed, so no-shows are discovered at the door

What we build

  • WhatsApp reservation journey: instant confirmation, D-1 and H-3 reminders with one-tap confirm or cancel
  • Auto-release to a waitlist, deposits for peak nights and groups
  • Resident campaigns — weekdays, summer, iftar — to your opt-in list
  • Villas: gap-night and long-stay offers, and a strong Google Business Profile

Your week after

Fewer surprises at the door, and quiet nights get filled on purpose.

Sleepless night 3 of 3 · Costs

03

Every month a new cost — and you only see your margin when the accountant calls.

Rent, salaries, Saudization, the new check-in rule: every month there's a new cost, and I don't know my real margin until the accountant calls.

— What we hear from hotel and restaurant operators

Why it hurts

  • UAE F&B salaries are up at least 20% in two years, and mall rents have no cap[1]
  • In KSA, hotel reception roles must be 100% Saudi from April 2026, and chefs 30% from March 2027[7]
  • Emaar requires an operator at the concierge for every holiday-home check-in[8]

What we build

  • Daily prime-cost and channel-margin dashboard from your POS, aggregator payouts and payroll
  • Food cost %, labour %, dish margin ranking
  • Holiday homes: per-unit P&L after OTA fees, cleaning, Tourism Dirham and DET fees
  • Guest-message automation — check-in, Wi-Fi, FAQs, Arabic and English — with human hand-over

Your week after

You see the problem on Tuesday, not at month-end close. Staff hours go to guests, not copy-paste.
