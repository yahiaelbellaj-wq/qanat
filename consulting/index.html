01

Stop selling for one month, and you have nothing in three.

If I stop selling for one month, we have nothing in three.

— What we hear from consulting founders

Why it hurts

  • In a vendor's poll of boutique firms, over half said 76–100% of new business comes from founders or partners (directional)[1]
  • Over 80% of prospects visit a firm's website before engaging — even when referred[2]
  • No follow-up cadence, no past-client reactivation, no case studies: feast, then famine

What we build

  • Pipeline in Sheets or HubSpot, fed by your website, LinkedIn and referrals
  • 3 anonymised case-study pages and a “book a diagnostic call” calendar
  • Quarterly reactivation emails to past clients and alumni
  • Founder LinkedIn calendar plus small ads to a named-account list
  • Monday pipeline digest emailed to the founder

Your week after

Monday starts with one page of deals, next steps and stale contacts. Follow-ups go out without you remembering.

Sleepless night 2 of 3 · Receivables

02

Delivered in March. Still chasing the invoice in July.

We delivered in March; I'm still chasing that invoice in July.

— What we hear from consulting founders

Why it hurts

  • In the UAE, B2B credit terms average 47 days, 58% of credit sales are paid late, and bad debt is 8% of overdue invoices[3]
  • Payments on some Saudi government engagements were reportedly pushed back in 2026 (the Ministry of Finance disputes it)[4]
  • Invoices go out late or get rejected for a wrong PO or a missing vendor number

What we build

  • Milestone-billing tracker linked to project status
  • Invoice draft triggered at client sign-off, with a PO and vendor-number checklist
  • Reminders at D-7, D0, D+7 and D+21 from “finance” — not from the partner
  • Payment links for private clients
  • Aged-receivables dashboard (0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+), e-invoicing-ready fields

Your week after

Nobody “remembers” to invoice. The partner only steps in on accounts over 60 days.

Sleepless night 3 of 3 · Utilisation

03

Your best people rewrite proposals on Thursdays instead of billing.

My best people spend Thursdays rewriting proposals instead of billing.

— What we hear from consulting founders

Why it hurts

  • Average billable utilisation fell to 68.9% in 2024[5] and 66.4% in 2025, against a 75% optimum (global benchmark)[6]
  • Every proposal written from scratch, with no go / no-go filter
  • Nobody sees billable vs non-billable hours per person until month-end

What we build

  • Proposal kit: intake form → first draft assembled from approved modules (method, bios, case studies, pricing)
  • Go / no-go scorecard before any RFP
  • Light timesheet and utilisation dashboard per person

Your week after

Proposals take hours, not days. Weak bids are declined early. Under-used people are visible on Friday, not at month-end.
