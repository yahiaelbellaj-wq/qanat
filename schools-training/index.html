Sleepless night 1 of 3 · Empty seats

01

A seat empty in September is money you never get back.

A seat empty in September is money I never get back — and a new nursery just opened on my street.

— What we hear from school owners and directors

Why it hurts

  • In Dubai, 38 new early-childhood centres opened while enrolment grew 8%; KHDA says capacity pressure has eased[1]
  • Groups with marketing budgets are consolidating — Taaleem agreed to buy 95% of Kids First (34 nurseries)[2]
  • Enquiries arrive across WhatsApp, Instagram and phone; families who toured but didn't enrol never hear back

What we build

  • Landing page per age group or course, with a fee range and a “book a tour” slot picker
  • WhatsApp confirmation and a 24-hour reminder for every tour
  • 3-step follow-up for families who toured: same-day thank-you with the fee sheet, D+3 answer to the top objection, D+10 seat or waitlist update
  • Enquiry → tour → registration tracker
  • Ads timed to the intake calendar, with regulator approval lead time built in

Your week after

Admissions works one list. You see the funnel on Monday instead of guessing.

Sleepless night 2 of 3 · Fees

02

Half the month chasing fees — and you can't send a child home over it.

Half my month is chasing fees, and I can't send a child home over it.

— What we hear from school owners and directors

Why it hurts

  • In a survey of 2,814 parents by a payments vendor, 88% feel strain when fees are due and half struggle to pay upfront[3]
  • Abu Dhabi bars punitive measures: three written warnings, no blocking exams[4]
  • Disputes end in court — one Dubai parent was ordered to pay Dh125,706[5]

What we build

  • Instalment schedule per family, generated at registration
  • Payment links, with an optional buy-now-pay-later partner
  • Neutral reminders at D-5, D0, D+3 and D+10, written within regulator rules — then the director
  • Overdue dashboard by family, with promise-to-pay notes
  • Re-enrolment deposit campaign

Your week after

Chasing becomes a 15-minute review, not a daily chore.

Sleepless night 3 of 3 · One phone

03

You're the admissions desk, the HR team and the inspection file — on one phone.

I'm the admissions desk, the HR department and the inspection file, all on one phone.

— What we hear from school owners and directors

Why it hurts

  • Parents prefer WhatsApp[6], and class groups can hit 50+ messages an hour[7]
  • Legal staff-to-child ratios run from 1:3 to 1:12 — one resignation can break a room[8]
  • TVTC logged 427 violations at Saudi training institutes in nine months of 2025, incl. late licence renewals[9]

What we build

  • Shared WhatsApp Business number with auto-answers for the top 20 FAQs
  • Absence and late-pickup forms, broadcast templates instead of group chaos
  • Staff and compliance tracker: visa, first-aid and safeguarding expiry alerts, permit dates
  • Daily ratio-by-room sheet and an inspection evidence folder
  • Teacher recruitment page with a screening form

Your week after

Routine questions stop reaching you. No surprise expiries. Vacancies are filled from a ready pool.
